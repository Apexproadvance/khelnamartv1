# Khelnamart — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Overview

**Product Name:** Khelnamart
**Tagline:** Bangladesh's largest toy marketplace
**Objective:** Build Bangladesh's first dedicated online marketplace where every toy retailer can sell products and customers can discover, compare, and purchase toys from anywhere in the country.

## 2. Vision & Mission

- **Mission:** Make every toy in Bangladesh discoverable and purchasable online.
- **Vision:** Become the default destination for buying children's toys in Bangladesh.

## 3. Problem Statement

### Customer Problems
- Cannot find specific toys nearby
- Have to visit multiple stores to compare
- Don't know which shop has stock
- No price comparison across stores
- Limited delivery outside major cities
- Difficult to find educational or age-appropriate toys

### Retailer Problems
- Most retailers don't have websites
- Limited online sales channels
- Difficult digital marketing
- Poor inventory visibility
- Depend on walk-in customers
- Compete with Facebook pages instead of structured commerce

## 4. Solution

Khelnamart is a marketplace connecting parents with toy retailers across Bangladesh:
- Marketplace for all toy retailers
- Individual seller dashboards
- Nationwide product discovery
- Secure online ordering
- Home delivery nationwide
- Inventory management
- Payment collection
- Customer reviews
- Marketing support

## 5. Marketplace Model

```
Customer → Khelnamart Website/App → Retail Store → Delivery Partner → Customer
```

## 6. Business Model (Revenue)

| Source | Description |
|---|---|
| Commission | 5–12% per order |
| Monthly Subscription | Premium seller plan |
| Featured Listings | Sponsored products |
| Homepage Promotions | Retailers pay for visibility |
| Brand Partnerships | Toy manufacturers |
| Advertising | Banner advertisements |

## 7. Target Users

### Primary — Parents (25–45)
Buying: birthday gifts, educational toys, Eid gifts, baby toys.

### Secondary — Gift buyers
Relatives, friends, corporate gifts.

### Sellers
Toy retailers, baby shops, book stores with toys, educational toy sellers, importers, brand distributors.

## 8. User Personas

**Parent:** "I need a LEGO set for my 8-year-old but don't know which store has it."
Needs: search, fast delivery, authentic products, reviews.

**Retail Store Owner:** "I want online sales without building my own website."
Needs: easy upload, order management, marketing, inventory.

Baby (0–2), Learning (2–5), STEM, Creative, Outdoor, Sports, Electronic, RC, Puzzle, Pretend Play, Collectibles, Books & Toys, Party Supplies, School Fun.

## 14. Search Filters
Age, Gender, Price, Brand, Material, Availability, Seller, City, Rating, Delivery Time, Discount.

## 15. Product Attributes
Name, SKU, Brand, Age, Category, Subcategory, Material, Color, Dimensions, Weight, Safety, Origin, Warranty, Stock, Images, Videos.

## 16. Trust Features
Verified sellers, original products, return policy, secure payment, customer support, verified reviews, store ratings.

## 17. Logistics
Delivery partners: Pathao, SteadFast, Paperfly, RedX, local courier, store pickup.

## 18. Payments
SSLCommerz, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Cash on Delivery.

## 19. User Journey

1. Parent visits Khelnamart
2. Searches "LEGO"
3. Filters Age = 6–8
4. Compares 5 sellers
5. Chooses best price
6. Adds to cart
7. Pays via bKash
8. Seller receives order
9. Packages item
10. Courier picks up
11. Customer tracks order
12. Receives product
13. Leaves review
